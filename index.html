🕎 How to Play Bible Scrabble

Rules

  • Take turns placing Bible words on the 11×11 board
  • The first word must cover the ★ center square
  • Every new word must connect to a tile already on the board
  • All words formed across & down must be valid Bible words
  • Your rack refills to 7 tiles after each play
  • Use ↩ Clear to return tiles to your rack, 🔄 Exchange to swap tiles with the bag (costs your turn), or ⏭ Pass to skip your turn
  • Game ends when the tile bag is empty

Board Squares

COVENANT×3 Word — entire word score tripled (corner squares) PROPHET×2 Word — entire word score doubled (diagonal squares) SCROLL×3 Letter — that tile's points tripled (edge squares) LAMP×2 Letter — that tile's points doubled (scattered squares) ★Star of Bethlehem — center square, first word starts here (acts as ×2 Word)
